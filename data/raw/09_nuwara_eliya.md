# Nuwara Eliya

## Overview
Nuwara Eliya, often called 'Little England', is a town in the central highlands of Sri Lanka. Known for its cool climate, colonial-era bungalows, and vast manicured tea estates, it offers a distinct contrast to the tropical lowlands.

## Best For
Cool weather, tea factory visits, and colonial architecture.

## Main Attractions
- **Gregory Lake**: A scenic lake offering boat rides, water sports, and lakeside dining.
- **Pedro Tea Estate**: A historic tea factory where visitors can learn about tea production and taste Ceylon tea.
- **Victoria Park**: A well-maintained public park filled with foreign flowers and bird species.
- **Post Office**: A charming, red-brick Tudor-style post office built in the late 19th century.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Temperatures can drop significantly in the evening; pack warm clothing. Tea purchases from factory shops make excellent gifts.*
