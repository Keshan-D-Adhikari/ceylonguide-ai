# Unawatuna

## Overview
Unawatuna is a coastal town near Galle, famous for its calm turquoise bay beach and lush green headlands. It is highly developed with beachside infrastructure and cafes.

## Best For
Swimming, snorkeling, scuba diving, and social beach dining.

## Main Attractions
- **Unawatuna Beach**: A banana-shaped beach protected by a reef, making it safe for year-round swimming.
- **Jungle Beach**: A secluded, quiet beach tucked away in the rainforest headland.
- **Japanese Peace Pagoda**: A beautiful white stupa built on the Rumassala hill with panoramic ocean views.
- **Dalawella Beach**: Famous for its iconic rope swing and scenic rock arches.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Snorkeling near Jungle Beach is excellent for spotting corals and sea turtles. Tuk-tuks can easily take you between Galle and Unawatuna.*
