# Kandy

## Overview
Kandy is a major city in the Central Province, nestled among scenic hills. It is the cultural capital of Sri Lanka and was the final capital of the ancient kings' era.

## Best For
Cultural immersion, religious history, and lush mountain scenery.

## Main Attractions
- **Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa)**: The most sacred Buddhist shrine in Sri Lanka, housing the relic of the tooth of Buddha.
- **Royal Botanic Gardens, Peradeniya**: A massive, beautifully landscaped botanical garden famous for orchids and palm avenues.
- **Kandy Lake**: A scenic artificial lake in the center of the city, perfect for peaceful walks.
- **Bahirawakanda Buddha Statue**: A giant white Buddha statue overlooking the city.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Dress modestly when visiting the Temple of the Tooth (shoulders and knees covered). The evening cultural dance performances are popular.*
