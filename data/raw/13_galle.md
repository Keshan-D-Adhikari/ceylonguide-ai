# Galle

## Overview
Galle is a historic coastal city situated in southwestern Sri Lanka. It is famous for its grand, ocean-facing fort built by European colonizers, which blends historical heritage with a trendy urban lifestyle.

## Best For
Colonial architecture, coastal walks, trendy boutiques, and dining.

## Main Attractions
- **Galle Fort**: A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring fortified ramparts, cobblestone streets, and colonial houses.
- **Galle Lighthouse**: An iconic, photo-worthy lighthouse located on the fort ramparts.
- **Dutch Reformed Church**: An 18th-century church with historical gravestones paving its floor.
- **Maritime Archeological Museum**: Housed in a Dutch warehouse, displaying marine relics.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Galle Fort is a pedestrian-friendly zone. It features excellent boutique cafes, gelaterias, and souvenir shops.*
