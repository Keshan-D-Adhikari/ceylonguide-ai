# Jaffna

## Overview
Jaffna is the capital city of the Northern Province, showcasing a unique and rich Tamil heritage, distinct cuisine, and historical relics of colonial and pre-colonial times.

## Best For
Tamil culture, historic Hindu temples, and unique northern cuisine.

## Main Attractions
- **Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil**: A grand, ornate Hindu temple known for its strict traditions and golden gopuram.
- **Jaffna Fort**: A large star-shaped fort built by the Portuguese and expanded by the Dutch.
- **Jaffna Public Library**: A beautiful, historic library symbolizing Tamil heritage.
- **Nainativu Island**: A nearby island housing the historic Nagadeepa Buddhist temple and a Hindu Kovil.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Try the famous Jaffna Crab Curry and sweet local mangoes. Respect local customs in kovils (men must remove shirts before entering Nallur).*
