# Yala National Park

## Overview
Yala National Park is the most visited and second largest national park in Sri Lanka, bordering the Indian Ocean. It is renowned for having one of the highest leopard densities in the world.

## Best For
Wildlife safaris, leopard spotting, and pristine dry-zone landscapes.

## Main Attractions
- **Leopard Safaris**: Guided open-jeep tours focusing on spotting the elusive Sri Lankan leopard.
- **Elephant & Bird Watching**: Large herds of elephants, spotted deer, crocodiles, and hundreds of bird species.
- **Sithulpawwa**: An ancient rock temple located deep within the park.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Safaris are run early in the morning and late in the afternoon. The park is typically closed for maintenance during September/October.*
