# Kalpitiya

## Overview
Kalpitiya is a coastal peninsula in the Puttalam District. It is Sri Lanka's premier kitesurfing destination and is also famous for marine wildlife like dolphins and whales.

## Best For
Kitesurfing, dolphin watching, and lagoon exploration.

## Main Attractions
- **Kitesurfing Lagoons**: Huge flat-water lagoons with steady winds, ideal for kitesurfing.
- **Dolphin Watching**: Morning boat tours to see large pods of spinner dolphins.
- **Dutch Fort & Church**: Historic colonial structures dating back to the Dutch period.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Kitesurfing season has two periods: May to September and December to March. Dolphin watching is best from November to April.*
