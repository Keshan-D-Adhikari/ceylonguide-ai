# Adam's Peak

## Overview
Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) is a sacred 2,243-meter mountain in central Sri Lanka. It is a major pilgrimage site where a rock formation near the summit is revered by multiple religions as a sacred footprint.

## Best For
Pilgrimage climbs, night hiking, and spectacular sunrises.

## Main Attractions
- **Sacred Footprint**: The shrine at the summit containing the footprint structure.
- **Sunrise shadow**: The triangular shadow cast by the mountain across the surrounding plains at sunrise.
- **The Stairs**: A challenging climb consisting of thousands of concrete steps.

## Suggested Duration
1 Day (Typically climbed overnight)

## Travel Notes
*The official pilgrimage season is from December to May. During this time, the trail is illuminated and lined with food stalls. Outside this season, the trail is dark and weather can be stormy.*
