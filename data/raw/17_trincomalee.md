# Trincomalee

## Overview
Trincomalee is a major port city on the northeastern coast of Sri Lanka. Built on a peninsula, it features natural deep-water harbors, sacred temples, and white-sand beaches.

## Best For
Pristine beaches, marine safaris, and Hindu culture.

## Main Attractions
- **Koneswaram Temple**: A sacred, colorful Hindu temple situated dramatically on a cliff edge overlooking the sea.
- **Nilaveli & Uppuveli Beaches**: Gorgeous stretches of white-sand beach with clear waters.
- **Pigeon Island National Park**: A marine sanctuary famous for pristine coral reefs, reef sharks, and sea turtles.
- **Fort Frederick**: A historic fort built by the Portuguese, now home to roaming deer.

## Suggested Duration
2 - 3 Days

## Travel Notes
*The best beach season for Trincomalee is from May to October, when the sea is calm. Pigeon Island is a must-visit for snorkeling.*
