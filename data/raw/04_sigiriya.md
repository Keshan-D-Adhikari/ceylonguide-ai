# Sigiriya

## Overview
Sigiriya, also known as the Lion Rock, is a magnificent ancient rock fortress located in the northern Matale District. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarks.

## Best For
Historical exploration, archaeological marvels, and dramatic hiking.

## Main Attractions
- **Sigiriya Lion Rock**: A colossal column of rock nearly 200 meters high, featuring ruins of an ancient palace on top and vibrant frescoes.
- **Pidurangala Rock**: A nearby rock offering panoramic views of Sigiriya Lion Rock, popular for sunrise climbs.
- **Mirror Wall & Frescoes**: Historic graffiti and beautifully preserved paintings of ancient maidens.
- **Sigiriya Gardens**: One of the oldest landscaped gardens in the world, featuring water, terrace, and stone gardens.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Climb early in the morning to avoid the midday heat and crowds. Wear sturdy shoes as the stairs near the top can be steep.*
