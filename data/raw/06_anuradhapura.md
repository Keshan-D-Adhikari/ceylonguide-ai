# Anuradhapura

## Overview
Anuradhapura is one of the ancient capitals of Sri Lanka, famous for its well-preserved ruins of ancient Sri Lankan civilization. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a sacred city in Buddhism.

## Best For
Archaeological tours, religious pilgrimage, and ancient ruins.

## Main Attractions
- **Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi**: The sacred fig tree, grown from a cutting of the historical Bodhi tree under which Buddha attained enlightenment.
- **Ruwanwelisaya**: A massive, majestic white stupa and one of the world's tallest ancient monuments.
- **Jetavanaramaya**: A colossal stupa that was once one of the tallest structures of the ancient world.
- **Isurumuniya**: A charming rock temple famous for its stone carvings, including the 'Isurumuniya Lovers'.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Renting a bicycle is a highly recommended and pleasant way to explore the vast ruins of the sacred city.*
