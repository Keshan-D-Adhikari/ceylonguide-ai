# Polonnaruwa

## Overview
Polonnaruwa was the second capital of Sri Lanka after the destruction of Anuradhapura. The ancient city is a UNESCO World Heritage site featuring remarkably preserved ruins and massive ancient reservoirs.

## Best For
Ancient architecture, royal history, and cycling tours.

## Main Attractions
- **Gal Vihara**: A rock temple containing four beautiful Buddha statues carved out of a single granite rock face.
- **Polonnaruwa Vatadage**: A circular relic house with intricate stone carvings and guard stones.
- **The Royal Palace**: Ruins of King Parakramabahu I's grand multi-story palace.
- **Parakrama Samudra**: A colossal man-made rain reservoir built to sustain the kingdom's agriculture.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Monkeys are common in the ruins; keep food and shiny objects secure. Like Anuradhapura, bicycle rentals are popular.*
