# Sinharaja Forest Reserve

## Overview
Sinharaja is Sri Lanka's last viable area of primary tropical rainforest. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and a biosphere reserve housing a massive percentage of Sri Lanka's endemic flora and fauna.

## Best For
Rainforest trekking, biodiversity observation, and birdwatching.

## Main Attractions
- **Rainforest Guided Hikes**: Walking trails led by local guides to spot endemic birds, reptiles, and frogs.
- **Waterfalls**: Inside the forest, several pristine streams cascade into beautiful waterfalls.
- **Giant Trees & Canopy**: High density of endemic dipterocarp trees and exotic orchids.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Leeches are highly active in the damp forest; wearing leech socks and carrying salt/spray is essential. Always hike with an authorized guide.*
