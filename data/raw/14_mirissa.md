# Mirissa

## Overview
Mirissa is a popular beach resort town on the southern coast. Famous for its crescent-shaped beach, whale watching excursions, and vibrant nightlife, it is a favorite among surfers and backpackers.

## Best For
Whale watching, beachside dining, surfing, and nightlife.

## Main Attractions
- **Whale Watching Tours**: Morning boat tours to spot blue whales, sperm whales, and dolphins.
- **Coconut Tree Hill**: A highly photographed red-clay dome covered in towering coconut palms.
- **Parrot Rock**: A small red-rock island just off the beach offering views of the shoreline.
- **Mirissa Beach**: Golden sands with vibrant beach bars and seafood restaurants.

## Suggested Duration
2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Whale watching season runs from November to April. Avoid climbing Parrot Rock during high tide as the waves can be dangerous.*
