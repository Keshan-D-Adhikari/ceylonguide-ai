# Negombo

## Overview
Negombo is a major coastal city situated close to Bandaranaike International Airport. Known for its wide sandy beaches, historic Dutch Canal, and massive fish markets, it is a convenient starting or ending point for travelers.

## Best For
Beach relaxation, lagoon boat tours, and seafood dining.

## Main Attractions
- **Negombo Beach**: A long stretch of sandy coastline lined with hotels and restaurants.
- **Dutch Canal (Hamilton Canal)**: A historic canal system built during Dutch rule, perfect for quiet boat rides.
- **Lellama Fish Market**: A busy fish market showcasing the traditional livelihood of local fishermen.
- **St. Mary's Church**: A grand church displaying beautiful ceiling paintings.

## Suggested Duration
1 Day

## Travel Notes
*Ideal for a relaxing stay immediately after arriving in the country. Boat tours on the Negombo Lagoon are great for bird watching.*
