# Bentota

## Overview
Bentota is a coastal resort town known for its long, golden sandy beaches, luxury resorts, and water sports opportunities along the Bentota River.

## Best For
Water sports, luxury resort stays, and relaxing river cruises.

## Main Attractions
- **Bentota Beach**: A wide, uncrowded sandy beach ideal for sunbathing and swimming.
- **Brief Garden**: The beautifully landscaped estate of renowned landscape architect Bevis Bawa.
- **Bentota River (Water Sports)**: Jet skiing, windsurfing, banana boating, and river safaris.
- **Kosgoda Sea Turtle Hatchery**: Located nearby, dedicated to turtle conservation and nesting.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*A river boat cruise along the Madu Ganga or Bentota River is fantastic for seeing mangroves and river monitor lizards.*
