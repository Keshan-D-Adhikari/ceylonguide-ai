# Ella

## Overview
Ella is a small, picturesque town in the highlands of Sri Lanka, surrounded by green hills, tea plantations, and dramatic mountain gaps. It is a haven for hikers and nature lovers.

## Best For
Scenic hiking, tea estate tours, and relaxed mountain vibes.

## Main Attractions
- **Nine Arch Bridge**: An iconic colonial-era stone railway bridge surrounded by lush green tea fields.
- **Little Adam's Peak**: An easy and rewarding hike offering breathtaking views of the Ella Gap.
- **Ella Rock**: A more challenging trek leading to a cliff edge with stunning panoramic views.
- **Ravana Falls**: A beautiful, easily accessible waterfall near the main road.

## Suggested Duration
2 - 3 Days

## Travel Notes
*The train ride from Kandy to Ella is universally considered one of the most scenic train journeys in the world. Book tickets early.*
