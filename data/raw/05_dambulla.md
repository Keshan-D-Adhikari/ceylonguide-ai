# Dambulla

## Overview
Dambulla is situated in the central part of Sri Lanka and is best known for its beautifully preserved cave temple complex. It forms part of the famous Cultural Triangle.

## Best For
Sacred cave art, ancient history, and spiritual journeys.

## Main Attractions
- **Dambulla Cave Temple (Golden Temple of Dambulla)**: A UNESCO World Heritage site containing five major caves filled with ancient statues and murals of Lord Buddha.
- **Golden Buddha Statue**: A giant gilded statue at the entrance of the temple complex.
- **Ibbankatuwa Megalithic Tombs**: An ancient prehistoric burial site indicating early civilization.

## Suggested Duration
1 Day

## Travel Notes
*Socks are recommended as you must walk barefoot on the rocky paths of the cave complex, which can get hot.*
