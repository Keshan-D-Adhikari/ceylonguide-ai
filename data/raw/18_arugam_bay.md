# Arugam Bay

## Overview
Arugam Bay is a world-renowned surf destination situated on the dry-zone southeast coast. It attracts a laid-back, bohemian crowd of international surfers and backpackers.

## Best For
Surfing, beach parties, and relaxed coastal vibes.

## Main Attractions
- **Main Point**: The primary surf break offering long right-hand point breaks.
- **Elephant Rock**: A scenic viewpoint and surf spot located south of the bay.
- **Kudumbigala Monastery**: An ancient forest monastery offering remote, quiet views.
- **Pottuvil Lagoon**: A peaceful lagoon tour for bird and crocodile watching.

## Suggested Duration
2 - 3 Days

## Travel Notes
*Surf season runs from May to September. Many shops and hotels operate seasonally, closing or reducing hours during the monsoon.*
