# Colombo

## Overview
Colombo is the commercial capital and largest city of Sri Lanka. It is a vibrant, bustling metropolis that blends modern skyscrapers with colonial-era architecture, diverse cultural sites, and a scenic coastal promenade.

## Best For
Urban exploration, shopping, food tasting, and historical architecture.

## Main Attractions
- **Galle Face Green**: A spacious ocean-side urban park popular for sunset walks and street food.
- **Gangaramaya Temple**: A famous Buddhist temple featuring eclectic architecture and a museum of artifacts.
- **National Museum of Colombo**: The largest museum in Sri Lanka, housing ancient royal treasures and historical exhibits.
- **Pettah Floating Market & Bazaar**: A lively local marketplace offering a wide range of goods and local street food.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*The Galle Face Green street food is highly recommended, especially the local wadey. Tuk-tuks are readily available, but use metered ones.*
