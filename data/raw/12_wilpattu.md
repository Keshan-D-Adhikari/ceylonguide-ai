# Wilpattu National Park

## Overview
Wilpattu is Sri Lanka's largest national park, famous for its unique natural lakes called 'Willus' that collect rainwater. It offers a peaceful and less crowded safari experience compared to Yala.

## Best For
Quiet wildlife safaris, leopard and sloth bear sightings, and pristine wilderness.

## Main Attractions
- **Willus (Natural Lakes)**: Rain-filled basins that attract a wide variety of wildlife.
- **Leopards & Sloth Bears**: Known for stable populations of leopards, elephants, and sloth bears.
- **Kudrimalai Point**: A historic coastal cliff site with copper-red soil.

## Suggested Duration
1 - 2 Days

## Travel Notes
*Because of its dense forest cover, spotting wildlife requires patience. Excellent for travelers seeking a serene safari experience.*
