# Horton Plains

## Overview
Horton Plains National Park is a protected area in the central highlands. It features montane grassland and cloud forests, situated at an altitude of over 2,000 meters.

## Best For
Cloud forest hiking, birdwatching, and panoramic views.

## Main Attractions
- **World's End**: A sheer precipice with a drop of about 880 meters, offering stunning views when clear.
- **Baker's Falls**: A famous and picturesque waterfall located along the main hiking loop.
- **Sambar Deer**: Large deer that are frequently seen grazing in the grasslands near the park entrance.

## Suggested Duration
1 Day (Morning excursion)

## Travel Notes
*Arrive by 6:00 AM to see World's End before mist and clouds roll in and block the view. Single-use plastics are strictly prohibited at the park entrance.*
